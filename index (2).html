Environmental Engineer. Researcher. Sustainability Advocate.

Gergino Chounna Yemele

I study how land subsidence, sea-level rise and groundwater extraction put coastal cities at risk, with a focus on the Gulf of Guinea. I connect engineering, research and strategy to build data-driven solutions for water, energy and environmental resilience in Africa.

Connecting science, business and policy

I am an Environmental Engineer from Cameroon, completing my Ph.D. at the University of Padova (DICEA). My work covers sustainability strategy, environmental analytics, and water and groundwater sustainability.

I want to help emerging economies, where institutional and technical voids slow progress, turn knowledge into action. I am complementing my engineering background with strategic management and international management.

Research focus

Land subsidence

Measuring sinking coastlines

Satellite SAR interferometry (InSAR) to quantify subsidence in Douala and link it to land-use change, urban expansion and subsurface geology.

See research outcomes
Coastal risk

Vulnerability of low-lying cities

Assessing how many people and assets along the Gulf of Guinea are exposed to elevation-based risk now and by 2050.

See research outcomes
Adaptation

Predicting and managing subsidence

Site-specific numerical and deep learning models, and mitigation strategies for Cameroon and similar coastal regions.

Mission

I want to contribute to a more sustainable and decent world through scientific research and development, improving access to water, energy and education. I also work to reduce the under-representation of scientists in the water sector and in adaptation to geo-environmental hazards.

Since 2022 I have represented the Rural Water Supply Network on the UNESCO Groundwater Youth Network steering committee, where I chair the communication committee.
